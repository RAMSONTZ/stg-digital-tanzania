Paediatrics · Respiratory

Non-severe pneumonia in children

Section 9.1.1.1 · PDF page 234 · Approval pending

Assessment / pathway

Confirm that pneumonia is non-severe and age is eligible before the antibiotic pathway.

Assessment / pathway

For non-severe pneumonia, the source links amoxicillin to treatment. Symptomatic antipyretics are conditional on fever and are alternatives—not mandatory combined therapy.

Follow-up

Original section advises giving the first dose in clinic and explaining home dosing and feeding.

Linked medicines

Amoxicillin ↗Paracetamol (only if fever) ↗Ibuprofen (alternative if fever) ↗

Obstetrics · Emergency

Primary postpartum haemorrhage

Section 11.5.1 · PDF page 304 · Approval pending

Assessment / pathway

Assessment / pathway

The source-linked tranexamic acid entry is only one component of management; it must not replace uterotonics, resuscitation and cause-directed measures.

Follow-up

Escalation, contraindications and timing require current obstetric protocol verification.
